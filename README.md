# Temper

A personal interval timer for iPhone. It runs Intervals, a Stopwatch or Open Rounds, speaks each Phase change, and saves each Workout of 1 minute or more to Apple Health.

The Apple Watch app is a companion that ships inside the iPhone app. Every Workout starts on the iPhone, which opens the Watch at Start. The Watch shows the running Workout with the heart rate and its Zone, taps the wrist at each Cue, and can pause, resume, mark Done and stop. It saves the Health workout with heart rate in place of the iPhone's copy. A Watch that arrives more than 10 s after Start shows "Running on iPhone" and records in the background. The iPhone app needs iOS 27 and the Watch app watchOS 26.
