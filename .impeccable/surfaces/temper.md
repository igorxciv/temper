## Scope

The iPhone app's screens: Plans home, the Plan board (Intervals, Stopwatch, Open Rounds, a Preset, a described Plan), Describe, the value sheet, the running screen, the complete screens, Settings and the Preset sheets. Visitor mode: Operate. The Watch app and Live Activity keep their current look until a later round.

## Audience and task

One owner now, the App Store later; gym and home. Before a Workout: one hand, seconds, pick or adjust a Plan and Start. During: phone metres away, read at a glance. After: confirm it went in.

## Constraints

Phase colors are a brand commitment. Native navigation and controls (NavigationStack, sheets, menus, glass buttons). Fixed sizes for times and Phase names, read at distance; iPhone SE at every text size; Increase Contrast variants for every ink.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Plan is written up on the board exactly as it will run, and ticked off as it runs. It refuses the category default: dark inset rows of timers with an activity ring.

OWN-WORLD: Gloss whiteboard ground #F6F7F5, black marker #1E2126, eraser ghost for Off and spent lines. Phase marker inks: Prepare #1F6FB2, Work #D9482B, Rest #13897A, Cooldown #4A55B8. SF Rounded bold; times large, tabular, in one right-hand column. Round-nib marker strokes, slightly wobbly with round caps, draw brackets, ticks and underlines, and never words. Start is a black-marker capsule. A running Workout flips to the flat dark Phase fields. Raised by the declined hand: every Plan visible on home as a whole cell (Event Catalog Grid); the running field given wholly to the time (Catalog Sleeve); every time in one tabular column (Exposure Record); flat Phase color, no gradients or tinted glass (Guide Map Lobes).

STORY: Temper opens on the last Plan. The owner sees the Workout as it will run, taps a line to change it, and Starts in one tap. Mid-set the Phase field reads from across the room. The Workout ends on the board with every line ticked, the total written under it, and whether it goes to Health.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The Plan board. Back to Plans, inline title naming the Plan, Presets menu at the trailing edge. Lines top to bottom: Phase name left, 34 pt bold rounded in its ink; time right, 52 pt tabular. A Rounds Block sits inside a marker bracket with "× 8" (or "× until Stop") at its left. An Off line is ghosted, an open line reads "until Done". One footnote line in secondary ink. "Start · 8:50" as a black capsule in the bottom bar, in the thumb zone. Signature interaction: on the complete screen each line is ticked by a marker stroke drawn in sequence; Reduce Motion shows the ticks at once.
