SAY THE WORKOUT, GET THE PLAN
Tell Temper what you're doing today, out loud or typed, the way you'd tell a training partner: "10 sets, open work, exactly 1 minute of rest between them." Apple Intelligence turns it into a plan on your iPhone and shows it as an outline you can check before you start. Name the exercises, and Temper says each one as it comes up. Your words never leave the device.

READABLE FROM ACROSS THE ROOM
The time fills the screen, and each phase has its own color: blue to get ready, coral for work, teal for rest, indigo for cooldown. One glance from the floor or the rack tells you where you are.

CUES YOU CAN HEAR
A boxing gong starts and ends every work period, or a softer chime or beep if you prefer. A voice names the cooldown and the end of the workout, and counts down the last five seconds of a timed phase aloud. Cues play over your music or podcast without pausing it or turning it down, and they keep playing with the iPhone locked or on silent.

THREE WAYS TO TRAIN
• Intervals: work and rest for a set number of rounds, with an optional prepare and cooldown. For HIIT, Tabata, EMOM and boxing rounds.
• Stopwatch: one work period that counts up past its target until you mark it done.
• Open Rounds: work for as long as a set takes, then a timed rest, for as many rounds as you like or a set number. For lifting and circuits.

Times run up to 30 minutes in 5-second steps, and rounds from 1 to 99. Save any Intervals, Stopwatch or Open Rounds setup as a preset and start it again in one tap.

NOTHING ENDS BY ACCIDENT
Open work ends only when you tap Done, and Stop always asks first.

ON THE LOCK SCREEN
A running workout stays on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with buttons to pause, resume and mark work done.

APPLE WATCH
Allow Heart Rate in Temper's Settings once, and every workout you start on your iPhone opens on your Watch. It shows the workout with your heart rate and heart-rate zone, taps your wrist at each phase change, and lets you pause, resume, mark done and stop from your wrist.

NOTHING LEAVES YOUR DEVICES
Temper doesn't connect to the internet. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party code. It saves nothing to Apple Health. While a workout runs on Apple Watch, the Watch records your heart rate and activity as it does for any workout, so your rings still move.

Requires iOS 27. Describe needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on, and understands English. The Apple Watch app needs watchOS 26.
