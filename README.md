# Temper

A personal training app for iPhone. It runs Intervals, a Stopwatch or Open Rounds, and announces each Phase change with a gong or spoken words. It saves nothing to Apple Health.

The Apple Watch app is a companion that ships inside the iPhone app. Every Workout starts on the iPhone, which opens the Watch at Start. The Watch shows the running Workout with the heart rate and its Zone, taps the wrist at each Cue, and can pause, resume, mark Done and stop. The heart rate comes from a workout session that saves no workout. A Watch that arrives more than 10 s after Start doesn't join the Workout and goes back to idle. The iPhone app needs iOS 27 and the Watch app watchOS 26.
