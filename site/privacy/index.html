Privacy Policy

Temper collects no data. Everything it keeps stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and none of it is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Last updated 16 November 2026.

In short

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code.
  • Temper doesn't connect to the internet. It has no server, so there's nowhere for your data to go.

What stays on your devices

Your Presets, the values you last used, and your settings are stored on your iPhone. They're included in your iPhone's own backup, like any app's data, and deleting Temper deletes them.

Apple Health

Temper asks for Health access only when you tap Allow Heart Rate in its Settings. It never asks when you start a Workout.

  • Read: your heart rate during a Workout on Apple Watch, and your date of birth and latest resting heart rate, which the Watch uses to work out your heart-rate Zones.
  • Written: nothing. Temper saves no workouts. It asks for permission to save workouts only because Apple Watch needs it to run the workout session that gives a live heart rate.

While a Workout runs on Apple Watch, watchOS itself records heart rate, active energy and exercise minutes in Health, as it does for any workout app.

What Temper reads from Health is used only on your devices, during the Workout, and is never shared, sold or used for advertising. Apple Health data is stored and managed by Apple. You can change or remove Temper's access at any time in the Health app, or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Microphone and speech

Temper uses the microphone only while you describe a Workout aloud on the Describe screen. Your speech is turned into text on your iPhone, and the audio isn't recorded or kept. iOS may download Apple's speech model the first time you use it.

Describe then turns your words into a Plan with Apple Intelligence's model on your iPhone. Your words aren't sent to Apple or anyone else.

Apple Watch

The iPhone and Apple Watch apps talk to each other directly, through Apple's connection between your paired devices.

Crash reports from Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics for Temper with the developer. Apple collects and anonymizes these; Temper itself sends nothing. You can turn this off in the same place.

Children

Temper collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. Every past version is kept in Temper's public source code repository.
