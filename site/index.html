Temper Support

Temper is an interval timer for iPhone. It runs Intervals, a Stopwatch or Open Rounds, and announces every Phase change aloud, so you never have to look at the phone mid-set.

Need help or found a bug? Email support@igorcodes.dev. Include your iPhone model and iOS version. In the app, Settings → Contact Support fills these in for you.

Common questions

Do Cues play with the iPhone locked or on silent?

Yes. A running Workout keeps going on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and its Cues play even with the Ring/Silent switch set to silent. Turn the volume up before you start, because Cues play at the iPhone's media volume.

Will Temper stop my music or podcast?

No. Cues play over your audio and never pause it or turn it down.

Why can't I use Describe?

Describe turns a Workout you say or type into a Plan using Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. It needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Right after you turn it on, the model can take a while to download. Describe understands English.

Does Temper save Workouts to Apple Health?

No. Temper saves no workouts. While a Workout runs on Apple Watch, the Watch itself records your heart rate, active energy and exercise minutes in Health, as it does for any workout app, so your rings still move.

How does Apple Watch work with Temper?

Start a Workout on the iPhone and Temper opens on the Watch. The Watch shows the running Workout, taps your wrist at each Cue, and can pause, resume, mark Done and stop. To see your heart rate and Zone, allow Heart Rate in Temper's Settings on the iPhone.

What does Temper do with my data?

Nothing leaves your devices. Temper has no account, no ads, no analytics and no network access. See the Privacy Policy.
